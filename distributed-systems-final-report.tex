\begin{abstract}
    The project consists in a simulation of a Cyber-Physical System, where some
    autonomous cars have to coordinate with each other to cross a roundabout,
    each from its starting point to its destination. Moreover, a central
    controller is available to give them more efficient commands, trying to
    maximize the intersection's throughput while always maintaining safety as
    the first priority. In fact, the main distributed systems challenge concerns
    the reliability and fault tolerance of the system as a whole: network and
    performance stability are also important, but failures, assumed to be
    inevitable, should never result into car accidents. Several measures have
    been implemented or just theorized in order to prevent deadlocks and
    starvation, to guarantee liveness, to coordinate together healthy vehicles
    with unreachable ones and to improve controller's predictions and
    evaluations in general.

    An application has also been produced to visualize the simulation in real
    time and control it through some commands.
\end{abstract}
