\section{Future works}\label{future-works}

As follows from the self-evaluation section (\ref{self-evaluation}), one of the
most urgent steps would be to fix the logic implementation problems and make the
simulation more correct (aiming at 0 collisions). Preventing the ``roundabout
deadlocks'' (\ref{arch:behaviour_controller}), especially with many disconnected
vehicles, would also be of high importance.

Then, as highlighted throughout the report, security hasn't been considered at
all: one could start from the suggestions in the design section
(\ref{arch:security}).

Once these fundamentals have been taken into account and solidly validated, the
development could proceed to add features, improve the controller's logic to
make it even more efficient and expand the simulation. Additional features may
include more road or roundabout lanes, obstacles on the road, different vehicles
or cars with different speed and acceleration parameters. For the controller,
better algorithms could be implemented. Finally, the simulation could be
expanded to a larger scale, for example integrating it into the context of more
roads in a city, which could lead to new distributed systems problems.
